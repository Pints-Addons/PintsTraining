Pint's Class Training - Changelog

v1.2.1
- Hunters can't use thrown weapons (warriors and rogues only): thrown weapons are no longer suggested or counted as
  usable for hunters.
- Weapon masters' tooltips (mousing over them, and their pins on Pint's Quest Book's map) say which weapon skills
  they'd teach you, which you already know, and which aren't for your class.

v1.2
- First release on CurseForge. A spellbook tab listing what your class trainer can teach you - available now, coming
  soon, not yet - with training costs, and which weapon masters teach the weapons you can use. WoW Forever's trainer
  spells and costs are included (ForeverTrainingData.lua). /ptrain opens it.
