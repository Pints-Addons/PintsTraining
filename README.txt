Pint's Training - what your class trainer can teach you, at a glance
(WoW Classic Era / WoW Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsTraining
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Adds a tab to your spellbook listing everything your class trainer has for you, wherever you are: what you can
  learn now, what's coming in the next few levels and what's further off, each with its training cost - so you know
  how much gold to bring before the trip. It also lists which weapon masters teach the weapon skills your class can
  use. WoW Forever's own trainer spells and costs are included.

  /ptrain - open it
